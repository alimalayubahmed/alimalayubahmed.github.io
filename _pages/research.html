Research Profile

My research lies at the intersection of accounting, emerging technologies, financial reporting, corporate disclosure, business intelligence, and digital scholarship. A central theme of my work is the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, distributed ledger systems, and data analytics to accounting, audit, financial transparency, compliance, and organizational decision-making.
