Academic and Research Profile

Prof. Dr. Alim Al Ayub Ahmed is an academic, researcher, editor, and consultant with more than two decades of experience in university teaching, research, academic leadership, scholarly publishing, and research development. His academic background is in Accounting and Information Systems, with research interests spanning financial reporting, corporate governance, artificial intelligence, blockchain, audit and compliance, business intelligence, digital scholarship, and research visibility.

He has served as Dean of the Faculty of Business and Director of the Institutional Quality Assurance Cell at Fareast International University, Bangladesh, and previously in professorial and research roles at the School of Accounting, Jiujiang University, China. Alongside his academic work, he remains active in editorial service, scholarly publishing, research visibility, and responsible academic communication.
