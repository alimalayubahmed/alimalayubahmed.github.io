--- title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- Teaching has been a central part of my academic career for more than two decades. My teaching experience spans accounting, business, research methodology, information systems, financial reporting, and technology-enabled business education across universities in Bangladesh and China.
## Teaching Experience I have more than 24 years of academic and teaching experience across higher education institutions in Bangladesh and China. My academic appointments have included: - Professor, School of Accounting, Jiujiang University, China - Associate Professor and Dean, Faculty of Business, Fareast International University, Bangladesh - Senior Faculty, ASA University Bangladesh - Faculty Member, Asian University of Bangladesh - Faculty Member, Pundra University of Science & Technology - Faculty Member, State Institute of Business Administration and Computer Science (SIBACS), Mymensingh My teaching responsibilities have included undergraduate and postgraduate instruction, student mentoring, research supervision, curriculum development, assessment, and academic quality assurance.
## Teaching Areas My major teaching and academic areas include: - Accounting - Accounting Information Systems - Financial Reporting - Corporate Governance - Research Methodology - Business Research - Data Analytics - Artificial Intelligence in Business - Blockchain and Emerging Technologies - Business and Management Studies
## Teaching Philosophy My teaching approach emphasizes the connection between theory, research, and practical application. I encourage students to understand not only the technical foundations of accounting and business disciplines, but also how emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, data analytics, blockchain, and digital information systems are transforming professional practice. I also place importance on research-based learning, critical thinking, academic integrity, and the development of independent analytical skills.
## Research Methodology and Academic Mentoring Research methodology has been an important part of my academic teaching and professional work. My teaching and mentoring activities include guidance on: - Research design - Literature review - Research questions and hypothesis development - Quantitative and qualitative research approaches - Data analysis - Academic writing - Scholarly publishing - Research ethics - Citation and research visibility - Academic profile development I have worked with students and researchers at different stages of academic research, from initial research design to publication and scholarly dissemination.
## Academic Leadership in Teaching and Quality Assurance My teaching experience has also been closely connected with academic leadership and institutional quality assurance. As Dean of the Faculty of Business and Director of the Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at Fareast International University, I was involved in academic planning, curriculum development, assessment, faculty coordination, quality assurance, and institutional academic activities. These responsibilities complemented my classroom teaching and provided broader experience in the development and management of higher education programs.
## International Teaching Experience My international academic experience includes serving as Professor at the School of Accounting, Jiujiang University, China. This experience provided opportunities to teach and work within an international academic environment, collaborate with faculty and researchers, and contribute to research activities involving Chinese academic institutions and research projects.
