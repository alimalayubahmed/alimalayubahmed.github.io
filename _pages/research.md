My research interests lie at the intersection of accounting, emerging technologies, corporate governance, financial reporting, and digital scholarship. My work increasingly focuses on the application of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Blockchain, and data analytics in accounting, governance, financial reporting, and organizational decision-making.

- Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology
- AI-Enabled Financial Reporting and Risk Analytics
- Audit, Compliance, and Financial Transparency
- Data Analytics for Governance and ESG
- Digital Scholarship and Research Visibility
